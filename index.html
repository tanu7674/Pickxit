Need a Non-Standard Photo Size?

Not every document fits a pre-loaded spec. Here's how to get an exact custom-size photo for any form, application, or ID card in under a minute.

1

3

Type your required Width (mm) and Height (mm) — any measurement from a form, embassy, or printer.

Convert & download
