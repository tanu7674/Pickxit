Custom Sizing
Need a Non-Standard Photo Size?

Not every document fits a pre-loaded spec. Here's how to get an exact custom-size photo for any form, application, or ID card in under a minute.

1

Upload your photo

Drop in any portrait photo in the Studio above — it stays on your device the whole time.

2

Select "Custom Size"

In Step 1 · Document Type, choose Custom Size from the "Other" group in the dropdown.

3

Enter exact dimensions

Type your required Width (mm) and Height (mm) — any measurement from a form, embassy, or printer.

4

Convert & download

Hit Convert — the AI crops and centers your face to those exact dimensions at 300 DPI, ready to print.
